Coil face, entering side. Debris packs the inlet and leaves the outside looking clean. 50HC-07-12 · p.41

Take the condensing temperature. More than 30 °F over ambient points here and rules out charge.

2

Fan rotation and blade pitch. Both turning isn't both working. A reversed fan after a motor swap moves almost no air. 50HC-07-12 · p.36

Subcooling against the chart. Read liquid line pressure and temperature, then compare. High subcooling with a clean coil means overcharge. R-410A PT chart · p.1
