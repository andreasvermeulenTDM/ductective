BRAND IDENTITY · V1

A clean, geometric identity for HVAC intelligence. The mark is the letter D extruded into a duct — you are looking straight down it. Detection without the cliché magnifying glass.

Primary lockup

The horizontal lockup is the default. Use the stacked version where width is constrained — app splash screens, square social avatars, embroidery.

Without tagline

Drop the tagline below roughly 160 px of lockup width, and anywhere the context already says what you do.

Single-colour

One-colour versions for laser etching, embroidery, single-plate print, faxed invoices and anywhere the gradient will not survive.

The mark & its alternates

Six concepts were drawn on the same 64 pt grid with matched stroke weights, so any of them can swap in without redrawing the system. Duct D is the recommendation.

Colour

Cool blue and steel. The gradient runs Signal Blue → Duct Blue on a 135° diagonal. Cyan Read is an accent only — it marks the thing the AI has found, never a background.

Usage

✓Keep clear space equal to the width of the mark's vertical stem on all four sides.
✕Do not rotate, skew, or add a drop shadow to the mark.
✓On photography, use the single-colour white lockup over a darkened area.
✕Do not recolour the cyan accent — it is the one fixed element in the system.
✓Minimum sizes: 120 px wide for the full lockup, 32 px for the full mark, 16 px for the simplified mark.
✕Do not place the gradient mark on a mid-blue background — switch to white or ink.
